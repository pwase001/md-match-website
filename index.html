Our Mission

What MD-Match Stands For

MD-Match was built by Dr. Philip Wasef, MD — an actively collaborating psychiatrist — to create a service that prioritizes both the NPs/PAs and the collaborating physician. The goals of MD-Match is to eliminate unnecessary costs for NPs/PAs, provide fair compensation to collaborating physicians, and ensure both parties remain in full compliance with board requirements. MD-Match was designed from the ground-up to appeal to both sides of the collaboration.

About MD-Match
MD-Match recruits board-certified, licensed physicians to serve as collaborating physicians for nurse practitioners and physician assistants. We handle the recruitment and matching process so you can focus on patient care. MD-Match does not set up medical director arrangements or physician ownership arrangements — we exclusively provide collaborating physicians for NPs and PAs.

We typically find a compatible collaborating physician within 7 days of your request. We maintain a roster of available physicians in multiple specialties and states, allowing us to move quickly.
Yes, every physician receives a background check. We do not include any physician who has a current or previous history within the past 5 years of any action against their license or medical malpractice claim. We can also assist with providing additional verification of state licensure on request. You will have an introductory meeting with your collaborating physician prior to signing of the collaborative agreement to personally ask any additional questions you may have.
Most of our physicians have DEAs in multiple states. We understand that in certain states a collaborating physician needs to have a state-specific DEA for the NP/PA to prescribe controlled substances, and we match accordingly.
Physician Availability & Response Times
We require all collaborating physicians to be available for at least one meeting per month. For urgent matters, they must respond within two hours. For routine questions or chart reviews, they must respond within 24 hours. If a physician consistently fails to meet these expectations, we will terminate that arrangement and find you a replacement at no additional cost.
We understand how critical continuity of care is. If a physician's license is suspended, revoked, or they are charged with a felony, you have the right to terminate immediately. For any situation — including emergencies — re-matching is always free and we will work to place you with a new physician within 7 days. Having MD-Match as your ongoing partner significantly improves your chances of a smooth transition compared to managing a private collaboration arrangement on your own. There are no payments owed to MD-Match during transition periods.

Malpractice Insurance
Both parties carry coverage. Your facility is required to maintain Professional Liability / Medical Malpractice Insurance with minimum limits of $1,000,000 per claim and $3,000,000 in the annual aggregate. MD-Match requests that NPs/PAs add the collaborating physician as an additional insured under your policy. The physician also carries their own malpractice policy for their private patient care.
In most cases, there is no change to the policy premium. Insurance carriers generally understand that certain providers require collaborating services and typically ask the collaborating physician to show proof of their own malpractice insurance — this ensures your policy only covers them for their role in your clinic.
Yes. MD-Match maintains a separate group malpractice policy to provide an additional layer of coverage for all collaborating physicians. This covers rare situations in which a claim is brought solely against the collaborating physician rather than the NP, PA, or clinic.
Please reach out directly to Dr. Wasef at philipwasef@md-match.com. The goal is not to provide lower collaborating costs just to have to pay more in insurance. Dr. Wasef will review your communication with your insurance carrier to ensure they understand we are only requesting simple additional insured coverage. If your insurance provider is indeed significantly increasing the yearly premium, we can omit this requirement since MD-Match still maintains a separate policy for collaborating physicians.
Regulatory Compliance
MD-Match assists with compliance checks and reminders for both the NP/PA and collaborating physician regarding state-specific requirements. We track compliance monthly via a direct check-in and compliance form with both parties. However, regulatory compliance — including your state's nurse practice act, physician practice act, and all applicable federal and state laws — is ultimately the sole responsibility of you and your collaborating physician.
No. MD-Match has no control over and does not direct the medical or clinical decisions of any collaborating physician or the NPs and PAs they supervise. All clinical and medical judgment remains entirely with the licensed professionals involved.
