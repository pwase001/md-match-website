Our Mission

What MD-Match Stands For

MD-Match was built by Dr. Philip Wasef, MD — an actively collaborating psychiatrist — to solve a problem he experienced firsthand: collaboration that works for everyone, not just one side of the table.

After years of collaborating with NPs and PAs across multiple states, Dr. Wasef recognized a gap: most platforms served one side of the arrangement while leaving the other navigating compliance alone. MD-Match was designed from the ground up to fix that — giving both physicians and advanced practice providers a structured, compliant, and fairly priced framework they can trust.

Dr. Philip Wasef, MD  ·  Founder & Actively Collaborating Psychiatrist
